It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or distributed by OpenAI or Tencent.
The installer does not replace or modify the Microsoft Store ChatGPT package.

and runs a local notification bridge under the current Windows user.

V1 sends Desktop Codex completion notifications to QQ. It does not promise
reliable QQ-to-Codex composer control.
